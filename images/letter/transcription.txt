[Letter text]
Pompton, N. J. Aug 9th / 61
R Borden Esq,
Dear Sir,
Mr J Horner
of N York informs me that
you were about to engage
in the manufacture of
fire arms, and advised me
to write you in regard
to a situation in connection
with their construction
I am familiar with
making the tools, and the
process of construction,
having had a practical
knowledge of the business,
I have been employed by
Mr Horner to take charge of the
machinery of his works in this
place, which are now stopped.
If you wish to know
further of my character
and ability, I would refer
you to Mr Horner,
Yours Respectly,
Wm. E. Morrison,

P.S. Please let me know as
soon as convenient whether
you would like to make
an arrangement with me,
W.E.M.
